Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI generate names?↓

NameForge AI uses a sophisticated algorithm with curated databases of 200+ name components including prefixes, roots, suffixes, power words, and industry-specific vocabulary. It combines these intelligently based on your description, style preferences, target audience, and selected industry to produce semantically meaningful and brandable names.

Are the generated names trademarked?↓

NameForge AI generates creative suggestions, but we strongly recommend conducting a trademark search before committing to any name. Use resources like USPTO (for the US), EUIPO (for Europe), or consult a legal professional. Domain availability shown is indicative only — always verify through a domain registrar.

Can I check domain availability?↓

Each generated name card shows a visual domain availability indicator (green = likely available, yellow = check required). However, these are estimated indicators. For a definitive answer, click through to a domain registrar like Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains to verify real-time availability.

Is this tool free to use?↓

Yes! NameForge AI is completely free to use with no account required. You can generate unlimited name combinations, save favorites to your browser, and copy names without any cost. We believe every entrepreneur deserves access to great naming tools.

How many names can I generate?↓

There's no limit! Each click of the Generate button produces 12 fresh, unique names. You can regenerate as many times as you like — the algorithm is designed to never repeat names within the same session, ensuring you always see fresh suggestions.

Can I save my favorite names?↓

Absolutely! Click the ❤️ heart button on any name card to save it to your favorites. Access your saved names via the floating favorites button at the bottom-right corner. Your favorites are stored in your browser's local storage and will persist between visits. You can also copy all favorites at once.
